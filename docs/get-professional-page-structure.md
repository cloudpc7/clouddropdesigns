## Project inquiry form (modal)

Opened by **Start a project**. Fields:

- Name, email
- Company, phone (optional)
- Project type, description
- Preferred date & time, additional availability notes
- Timeline, budget range (optional)

Submit opens email to `cloud@clouddropdesigns.com` with details pre-filled (same pattern as Drop & Elevate).

## Principles (Working with me)

- Understand the business problem before writing the first line of code.
- Ship polished, scalable work — not prototypes that become permanent.
- Stay accessible from first conversation through launch and beyond.
